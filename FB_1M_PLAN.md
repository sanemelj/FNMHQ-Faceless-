## Rules for reach (what Facebook pushes)
- **Original content only.** No match footage or TV clips. A copyright claim breaks "follow our policies" and kills the invitation. No reposting other pages' memes or images.
- Native upload to Facebook (no TikTok watermark). Vertical 9:16. Reels 20–40 s, topic on frame 0. Photo posts 1080×1350.
- Tier-1 first: Messi, Ronaldo, Real, Barça, Man United, Liverpool, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, PSG. They have the biggest Facebook audiences.
- **Speed:** results card within 30 min of FT on big matches. That's why Claude checks every 30 min.
- **Feed a breakout:** a post doing ≥3× the median in 6 h gets 2 follow-ups on the same topic the same day (Haiti Brief rule).
- Captions: one real question, 3 hashtags max. No "like and share" begging. Facebook demotes engagement bait.
- AI label: Yes only on avatar videos.
