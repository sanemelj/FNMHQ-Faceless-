# FNMHQ — newsroom profile (newsroom-journalism skill, Sam Nov 18 2026: "pale tankou yon jounalis")

- **Platforms:** YouTube @FootballNewsMemesHQ · Facebook FNMHQ page · TikTok @footballnewsmemeshq.
- **Language and audience:** English, US-first (plus worldwide football fans); mostly on mobile; Facebook = the 1M-views goal (`FB_1M_PLAN.md`).
- **Beat, in priority order:**
  1. Today's biggest stories for the biggest fanbases: Real, Barça, Man United, Liverpool, Arsenal, Chelsea, City, PSG, Bayern, Messi, Ronaldo, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, USA (Haiti when it's news).
  2. Results and big moments: last-minute winners, upsets, hat-tricks, sackings and hires.
  3. Previews of tonight's big games.
- **Red lines:**
  - No gossip about private lives, no rumours presented as fact, no match or TV footage, no invented quotes or "records".
  - Never mock a fanbase, a nationality or a people. Criticise performances, decisions and clubs only.
- **Trusted sources:**
  - Club, league and federation sites; ESPN, BBC Sport, Sky Sports, AP/Reuters (wire = 1 source), The Athletic, Goal, Concacaf.com, U.S. Soccer.
  - Spanish-language: Récord, Mediotiempo, Infobae, Marca, AS.
  - Stats: Opta/FBref/Transfermarkt (one provider = 1 source).
- **Single attributed reporters (transfers):** tier-1 only, always "according to …". "Done deal" only with an official announcement.
- **Schedule (CT):** Shorts 7 AM (THE NUMBER), 12 PM, 5 PM · FB photos 9 AM, 2 PM, 8:30 PM · long-form 7 PM Mon/Wed/Fri. Every clip has the avatars.
- **Presenters:** Callum Pryce (anchor) and Jamie Hollis (analyst). They're professional sports journalists, not fans. Their lines:
  - Short sentences, contractions, names, numbers. Cite once, then tell the story.
  - Opinion is labelled ("Our take").
  - One natural human joke at most per segment.
  - No disclaimers, no "let's dive in", no "reportedly" in every line.
- **Owner decisions (Sam):** any real doubt about a fact or a clip, money and monetization, strikes and claims, deleting or reposting.
- **Covered-topics log:** `COVERED.md` · **Lessons:** add to the project plan + this file.

## Pre-publish check (every script, caption and title)
1. Every fact has 2 independent sources (wire = 1), or 1 official source, or it is attributed ("X reports").
2. Dates checked, weekday and time zone included. Live scores only after the final whistle is confirmed.
3. Numbers: no "record", "first", "worst" without a stats source. Simple arithmetic from confirmed figures is OK.
4. Quotes: exact words, date and place.
5. Title: the new concrete fact plus the name people search; true, no fake quotes, no emoji spam.
6. Credits for every photo; opinion labelled.
