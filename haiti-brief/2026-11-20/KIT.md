**Deskripsyon:**

El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele registered on Thursday, Nov. 19, at the Supreme Electoral Tribunal to run for a third consecutive term in the February 2027 election. Last year, his party used its supermajority in Congress to remove presidential term limits and extend terms from five to six years. "In El Salvador there is total, complete open democracy," Bukele told supporters. Human Rights Watch's Juan Pappier told the AP: "Bukele is running for a third consecutive term after packing the court, rewriting the constitution to his liking..."

Last month, Bukele sent about 2,500 soldiers to Haiti after four Salvadoran members of the UN-backed Gang Suppression Force went missing. Thousands of Haitians have marched in support; many analysts question his motives.
