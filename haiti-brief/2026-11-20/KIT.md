**Deskripsyon:**

```
El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele registered on Thursday, Nov. 19, at the Supreme Electoral Tribunal to run for a third consecutive term in the February 2027 election. Last year, his party used its supermajority in Congress to remove presidential term limits and extend terms from five to six years. "In El Salvador there is total, complete open democracy," Bukele told supporters. Human Rights Watch's Juan Pappier told the AP: "Bukele is running for a third consecutive term after packing the court, rewriting the constitution to his liking..."

Last month, Bukele sent about 2,500 soldiers to Haiti after four Salvadoran members of the UN-backed Gang Suppression Force went missing. After registering, he said his troops are giving Haiti's gangs "a lesson, as we gave it to the gangs here" (EFE, translated from Spanish). Thousands of Haitians have marched in support; many analysts question his motives.
```

**Deskripsyon:**
```
A six-year-old U.S. citizen kidnapped in Port-au-Prince on June 11 was released on Monday, Nov. 16, after about five months in captivity, the Miami Herald reports. She was taken with her mother and her father, James Boyard, a senior Haitian National Police official who serves as chief of staff at Haiti's defense ministry. Her mother was freed weeks later, reportedly to arrange ransom payments.

On Thursday, Nov. 19, U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio wrote on X: "Thank you to the Haitian National Police, the Anti-Kidnapping Unit, and U.S. law enforcement for securing the release of a kidnapped American minor." No details have been given on how she was freed. Her father is still being held along with four other police officers, according to the Miami Herald.
```
