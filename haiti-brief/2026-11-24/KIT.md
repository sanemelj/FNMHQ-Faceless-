**Deskripsyon:**
```
In a letter dated Monday, Nov. 23, Sen. Jeanne Shaheen, the top Democrat on the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, and Rep. Gregory Meeks, the top Democrat on the House Foreign Affairs Committee, told Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth that the administration is failing to carry out the Haiti Criminal Collusion Transparency Act. The law, enacted in December 2025, required sanctions on the political and economic sponsors of Haiti's gangs by Sept. 28, 2026. The lawmakers say that deadline was missed and that the first report listed only people who had already been sanctioned. They ask for immediate steps to comply with the law and a bipartisan classified briefing. The AP reported no response from the administration.
```
