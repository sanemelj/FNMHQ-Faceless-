Haiti's health minister says the State University Hospital of Haiti — the General Hospital, the capital's biggest public hospital — will resume services starting Dec. 2, at about 50 to 60 percent of its capacity. It has been closed since gang attacks on downtown Port-au-Prince on Feb. 29, 2024. A government delegation and reporters went back inside on Nov. 23 and found it badly damaged and looted. This week, Haiti's Secretary of State for Communication, Lucien Jura, said the Salvadoran troops will stay in Haiti until October 2027 under the Haitian government's leadership — about seven months past the end of the UN mandate for the Gang Suppression Force. We also cover the overnight police operation in Martissant 2B and the deadly attack on a market in Bongars, Kenscoff.
