**Deskripsyon:**
```
Haiti's Justice and Public Security Minister Patrick Pélissier told reporters in San Salvador on Tuesday, Nov. 17, that there is "no date" for Salvadoran troops to leave Haiti, the Associated Press reports. He said their support is needed to reinforce Haiti's police at least until elections are held next year, and that officials will work on a legal framework so the Salvadoran forces can stay longer. A day earlier, El Salvador's Vice President Félix Ulloa and Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro said the soldiers would go home once four missing aviation technicians are found. Haiti's first round of elections is set for July 18, 2027. El Salvador's government did not immediately respond.
```
